The problem

Your cash is reconciled
days after it matters.

Multi-channel retail creates a reconciliation problem that gets worse as you scale. More PSPs, more settlement schedules, more manual work.

01
Settlements arrive on different schedules
Stripe settles daily. Amazon every 14 days — with reserves. PayPal holds funds unpredictably. Your bank receives them all at different times with no standard reference. Matching them is a manual job that falls to your finance team.
02
Your cash position is always out of date
By the time your team has reconciled last week's settlements, this week's have already started arriving. Your Finance Director makes cash decisions based on data that's 48-72 hours stale. At scale, that gap is material.
03
Discrepancies hide in the detail
PSPs net fees, refunds, and FX adjustments against your payouts without clear itemisation. A £40,000 settlement arrives as £37,200 and your team spends hours tracing the difference — time that should be spent on analysis, not administration.
04
No forward visibility when you need it most
Xero tells you what happened last month. Your bank tells you what's there today. Neither tells you what your cash position will be in 14 days when your next Amazon disbursement is due and your supplier payment goes out. That gap costs you sleep and sometimes costs you money.
How it works

Connected, reconciled,
and live in 48 hours.

No lengthy implementation. No consultants. Shodipo connects to your existing PSPs and banking relationships and starts reconciling immediately.

01
Connect your payment platforms
Authorise Shodipo to read data from Stripe, Amazon Seller Central, PayPal, and Shopify Payments using secure OAuth connections. Read-only access — Shodipo never touches your money, only reads the data. Takes under 20 minutes per PSP.
02
Connect your bank via Open Banking
Using FCA-regulated Open Banking infrastructure, Shodipo connects to your business bank accounts — Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, and others. This is the connection that closes the loop between what your PSPs say is coming and what actually lands.
03
Reconciliation runs automatically
Shodipo matches every PSP settlement against your bank credits in real time. Matched settlements are confirmed automatically. Discrepancies are flagged with the exact difference and probable cause. Your team reviews exceptions — not every transaction.
